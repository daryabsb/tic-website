7. Multi-Country Localization, Maps & Contact Architecture

Global Local Pages & Hreflang Strategy
To capture local search queries across key import/export markets (UAE, Iraq, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, China, India,
Europe), TICQC will deploy dedicated country pages with full localization:

  · Country-Specific Landing Pages: E.g., /coc/iraq, /coc/egypt, /location/uae featuring local regulatory
    body references, local port coverage, and local office contact numbers.

  · Embedded Interactive Location Maps: Every local country page features an interactive Google Map displaying the
    local TICQC office/lab address or regional coverage area.

  · Hreflang Implementation: Strict multi-regional HTML header tags to prevent cross-border duplicate content
    penalties:

      <link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-ae" href="https://ticqc.com/uae" />
      <link rel="alternate" hreflang="ar-iq" href="https://ticqc.com/coc/iraq" />
      <link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-eg" href="https://ticqc.com/coc/egypt" />
      <link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://ticqc.com/" />

Unified Contact Page with Location Map Matrix

A central Contact Us page ( /contact ) consolidates all worldwide physical presence points onto a master interactive
location map. Visitors can filter offices by region (Middle East, Asia-Pacific, Africa, Europe), view exact physical
addresses, phone lines, email desks, and submit RFQs directly routed to the nearest regional inspection desk.
