# TÜV group websites teardown: TÜV SÜD (tuvsud.com), TÜV Rheinland (tuv.com), TÜV NORD (tuev-nord.de / tuv-nord.com)

Research date: 2026-10-21. Method: live fetches of the sites (WebFetch, curl, and the Chrome extension for tuvsud.com, which blocks automated fetchers behind a Cloudflare challenge), HTTP header and HTML-source inspection, and web searches. Wayback Machine was not reachable from this environment. Screenshots of tuvsud.com could not be captured (renderer time-outs), so visual-design observations for that site are based on the DOM, source and the company's own brand pages, not on a rendered view.

Access notes (for the report writer):
- tuvsud.com returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch and to curl (Cloudflare "Just a moment..." challenge page, `Server: cloudflare`, `cf-cache-status`). Content was read through the Chrome extension instead.
- tuv.com and tuev-nord.de / tuv-nord.com fetched normally.
- web.archive.org is blocked in this environment, so no historical comparisons were possible.

---

## Key question 1: Global information architecture (top-level menu, mega-menu, Services vs Industries vs Solutions, depth, breadcrumbs, search)

### Takeaway
All three sites separate the catalogue along at least two axes (what we do = services; who we do it for = industries), and the two most recently rebuilt sites (TÜV SÜD global template, TÜV NORD 2023 relaunch) add a third axis of "topics". TÜV SÜD is the deepest and most structured (Solutions > By Industries / By Services / By Focus Topics, with an in-menu service search); TÜV Rheinland is the flattest (five service-category menu items, no visible mega-menu content on the world site, heavy reliance on on-page card grids and site search).

### Cited Findings
- **TÜV SÜD global site (new template, /en):** top-level menu items are "Solutions", "Careers", "Company", "Newsroom", "Resources", "Sustainability", plus a portal link to `https://myportal.tuvsud.com/`, a "Global | EN" site/language button and a "Search" button. — [tuvsud.com/en, read via Chrome accessibility tree](https://www.tuvsud.com/en)
- **TÜV SÜD "Solutions" mega-menu** has three sub-axes: "By Industries", "By Services", "By Focus Topics". Under By Industries: Automotive, Building & Construction, Chemical Processing, Consumer Products & Retail, Energy, Manufacturing, Medical Devices, Rail. Under By Services: Auditing, Inspection, Management System Certification, Product Certification, Technical Advisory, Testing, Training, Verification & Validation. Each third-level panel shows a "Learn more" link to the hub page, a text box "Search services in Automotive" / "Search services in Auditing", 4-5 featured service links, and "View all services" pointing to an anchor `#solutionfinder` on the hub page (e.g. `/en/services/auditing#solutionfinder`). — [tuvsud.com/en](https://www.tuvsud.com/en)
- **TÜV SÜD service URL pattern:** `/en/services/{service-family}/{service}` (e.g. `/en/services/auditing/social-compliance-audits`) and `/en/industries/{industry}/{service}` (e.g. `/en/industries/automotive/iso-26262-for-automotives`), i.e. three levels deep; breadcrumbs on a service page read Home > Auditing > Social compliance audits. — [tuvsud.com service page](https://www.tuvsud.com/en/services/auditing/social-compliance-audits)
- **TÜV SÜD search-snippet view of the same menu:** "Solutions" organised by industry, by service type and by focus topic (AI, cybersecurity, hydrogen, Industry 4.0); "Company" = About Us, corporate governance, publications (annual and sustainability reports), compliance (code of conduct, trust channel); "Resources" = Knowledge Hub, Events and Webinar Hub, Customer Hub (certificate explorer, conformity marks explorer), "View All Resources". — [tuvsud.com/en-us/services (search snippet)](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-us/services)
- **TÜV SÜD regional sites still on the OLD template** (e.g. the Middle East site /en-ae) use a different, older menu: "INDUSTRIES & SERVICES", "RESOURCES", "ACADEMY STORE", "ABOUT", "CAREERS", "PRESS AND MEDIA", "SITE SELECTOR", "LOCATIONS", "CONTACT US", plus "Search". So two different IAs coexist on one domain. — [tuvsud.com/en-ae](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae)
- **TÜV Rheinland world site (/world/en and /en):** the main menu has five service categories: "Testing & Assessment", "Certification & Auditing", "Training & Qualification", "Inspection & Supervision", "Consulting & Project Management"; a "Company" dropdown with "about us", "contact", "jobs and career", "locations", "press and media". The five category links point to "#" and no sub-items are present in the markup fetched, so the mega-menu content (if any) is injected client-side. — [tuv.com/en](https://www.tuv.com/en)
- **TÜV Rheinland homepage body does the navigation work:** a "Featured Services" grid of ~25 cards with a filter bar by industry ("Traffic, Vehicles & Rail", "Information Security & Telecommunications", ...), "Hot Topics" tiles, "Selected Services", "Most Searched Terms" chips ("Functional Safety", "Certipedia", "Low Blue Light", "ISO 9001", "Tisax"). — [tuv.com/en](https://www.tuv.com/en)
- **TÜV Rheinland service URLs** are flat: `/world/en/iso-9001-certification.html` with breadcrumbs Home > Quality > Quality Management > ISO 9001 Certification (the breadcrumb hierarchy is deeper than the URL). — [tuv.com ISO 9001 page](https://www.tuv.com/world/en/iso-9001-certification.html)
- **TÜV NORD (tuev-nord.de/en, 2023 relaunch):** top-level items "Services", "Industries", "Topics", "Knowledge" (/en/knowledge/), plus "Customer login" and an "Open search" control. Services mega-menu has four columns: "Audit and expertise" (7 sub-areas incl. "Vehicles and rail", "Energy and safety", "Psychological Assessment" + a Spotlight group), "Driving licence and vehicle", "Auditing and Certification" ("Management systems", "Product certifications", "Individual certifications"), "Consulting". Industries mega-menu: six categories each with a short description and 1-4 sub-links (e.g. "Metal and electrical industry", "Railway industry", "Pharmacy", "Data Centers"). Topics mega-menu: five categories (e.g. "Functional Safety", "Artificial Intelligence", "Hydrogen", "Smart Home"). — [tuev-nord.de/en](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/)
- **TÜV NORD URL depth:** `/en/services/auditing-and-certification/iso-9001/` (3 levels) and product certifications even deeper: `/en/services/audit-and-expertise/product-certification/electromagnetic-compatibility-emc/electrical-safety/` (5 levels). Breadcrumbs: Home > Services > Auditing and Certification > ISO 9001 Certification. — [tuev-nord.de certification hub](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/company/certification/)
- **TÜV NORD hub pages have a filterable catalogue:** "All our audit and certification services" with filter categories (System certification, Product certification, Verification, Validation, Certification of persons) and "Reset filter". — [tuev-nord.de certification hub](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/company/certification/)
- **TÜV NORD's older regional template** (tuv-nord.com/middleeast, "TÜV Middle East W.L.L.") uses a different path scheme: `/middleeast/en/services/industrial-services/`, `/middleeast/en/our-company/mission-and-vision/`. — [search results, tuv-nord.com/middleeast](https://www.tuv-nord.com/middleeast/en/services/industrial-services/)

### Inferences
- The strongest common pattern: a single "Solutions/Services" entry point split by Industry and by Service, with topic/"hot topic" landing pages as a marketing layer on top. TIC's current split (Industries vs Services as separate top-level items) is consistent with TÜV NORD and TÜV SÜD, but neither big player puts "Government Services Programs" at menu level; they bury export-certification in a dedicated hub (see KQ5).
- An in-menu "search services in X" box (TÜV SÜD) and a filterable "all services" catalogue on hub pages (TÜV NORD, TÜV SÜD "#solutionfinder") are the two mechanisms used to keep mega-menus short while catalogues stay large. For a 13-office company with a modest catalogue, a filterable hub page is enough.
- TÜV SÜD's mixed templates on one domain show the cost of a multi-year migration; TIC should plan a single template from day one.

### Gaps
- The client-side content of TÜV Rheinland's five service-category dropdowns could not be captured (links are "#" in the fetched HTML).
- TÜV SÜD "By Focus Topics", "Company", "Resources" and "Sustainability" menus were not expanded in the browser session (only Solutions > By Industries and By Services were read); their contents are taken from search snippets.

## Key question 2: Country / region / language handling

### Takeaway
All three use a country-first selector (country then language) and give each country its own sub-site with its own homepage, but differ in how local the local pages are: TÜV Rheinland's UAE site is a full country site (EN + AR) sharing the world template; TÜV SÜD's Middle East site is a regional site (/en-ae) covering six GCC/Egypt countries on an older template; TÜV NORD runs a very thin Middle East sub-site (3 menu items) and a separate country-selector landing page at tuv-nord.com.

### Cited Findings
- **TÜV Rheinland:** header shows "World Site / EN" with the prompt "Choose country/ region and language"; the selector lists roughly 80 countries, some with multiple languages (Belgium FR/NL, Morocco EN/AR/FR, Tunisia EN/AR/FR). — [tuv.com/en](https://www.tuv.com/en)
- **TÜV Rheinland UAE site** is at `/united-arab-emirates/en/` with an "AR" option; its menu keeps the same five service categories and a "Company" menu with "jobs and careers" and "about us". It contains region-specific cards ("Approvals for the Middle East", "DEWA Outdoor Extension", "Services for the Oil and Gas Industry", "150 Years of TÜV Rheinland") but lists no UAE office addresses on the homepage; addresses are on a separate "offices" page. — [tuv.com UAE home](https://www.tuv.com/united-arab-emirates/en/)
- **TÜV Rheinland UAE offices page** is a plain vertical list of four offices (Abu Dhabi Khalidiyah, Dubai Airport Free Zone, Jafza, Mussafah) with address, phone, fax and an "Email" link that goes to the global contact form; no map, no email addresses, and offices 1 and 4 share the same phone/fax/P.O. Box. — [tuv.com UAE offices](https://www.tuv.com/united-arab-emirates/en/about-us/united-arab-emirates-t%C3%BCv-rheinland-middle-east/offices/)
- **TÜV Rheinland** has been in the UAE since 2007 with offices in Dubai Airport Free Zone (+971 4 233 9000), Jebel Ali Free Zone (+971 4 8800669) and Abu Dhabi; the local site mentions DEWA choosing TÜV Rheinland for extended outdoor testing of solar modules. — [tuv.com UAE about page (search snippet)](https://www.tuv.com/united-arab-emirates/en/about-us/united-arab-emirates-t%C3%BCv-rheinland-middle-east/)
- **TÜV SÜD:** the global site uses a "Global | EN" button; regional sites use locale paths such as `/en-ae`, `/en-gb`, `/en-us`, `/th-th`, `/sl-si`, `/es-es` and a "SITE SELECTOR" menu item. — [tuvsud.com/en](https://www.tuvsud.com/en); [tuvsud.com/en-ae](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae)
- **TÜV SÜD Middle East site (/en-ae)** is one regional site titled "TÜV SÜD in Middle East – Add value. Inspire trust." Its hero carousel includes six country tiles "IN SAUDI ARABIA / IN UAE / IN QATAR / IN BAHRAIN / IN OMAN / IN EGYPT", each with its own "CONTACT US" button, plus "100+ TRAINING COURSES NOW AVAILABLE TO BE BOOKED ONLINE" ("BOOK ONLINE"), "HYDROGEN SERVICES FOR A CLEAN FUTURE", "BLOGS ON SUSTAINABILITY By Niranjan Nadkarni, CEO of TÜV SÜD South & South-East Asia, Middle East & Africa Region", and annual/sustainability report tiles. — [tuvsud.com/en-ae](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae)
- **TÜV SÜD Middle East facts:** head office in Mussafah, Abu Dhabi (+971 2 6767600) and a Business Bay office in Dubai (+971 4 447 3113); in the Middle East since 2001; offices in six countries; MOIAT Notified Body (0017). — [tuvsud.com en-ae about/uae (search snippets)](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/about-us/tuv-sud-in-middle-east/uae); [imprint](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/imprint)

- **TÜV NORD:** tuev-nord.de/en has a "Go to Country Selector page" link (to tuv-nord.com/en/), a "Global" link, and a language menu "German" / "English". — [tuev-nord.de/en](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/)
- **tuv-nord.com/en is purely a country-selector landing page**: hero "TÜV NORD worldwide / Global expertise, local services. / Find your local service center. Worldwide.", then country lists grouped Europe A–G, Europe H–Z, Asia & Pacific, Africa, The Americas, each linking to a country sub-site (UAE = `/me/en/`, Qatar = `/qa/en/`, Egypt = `/eg/en/`, India = `/in/en/`). Data-quality defects observed: Bosnia and Herzegovina links to the Bulgaria site; Norway and Sweden share one URL and the Sweden link text says "TÜV NORD Switzerland"; USA (ALTER) has no URL. — [tuv-nord.com/en](https://www.tuv-nord.com/en/)
- **TÜV NORD Middle East (/me/en)** menu is only "Services" (Overview, Certification, Global Market Access, Amusement Ride Inspection, Training > Data Center Trainings), "Company" (Overview, About TÜV NORD Middle East, Contact us) and "Downloads"; language menu shows only English; hero "Inspired by Knowledge." / "TÜV NORD Middle East"; no office addresses on the homepage. — [tuv-nord.com/me/en](https://www.tuv-nord.com/me/en/)

### Inferences
- The convention across all three: one global/"world" site plus country sub-sites under the same domain with a path prefix (`/united-arab-emirates/en/`, `/en-ae`, `/me/en/`), not separate domains. For TIC (13 offices, one brand), a single domain with office/country pages, and English plus Arabic as the first language pair, matches the TÜV Rheinland UAE pattern.
- Local pages that carry only generic content (TÜV NORD ME, TÜV SÜD ME old template) look weaker than the global site; the useful local content is addresses, local accreditations/notified-body status, local programmes and local events.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether TÜV Rheinland's Arabic UAE site is a full translation or partial.
- Country-detection / geo-redirect behaviour (auto-suggesting the visitor's country site) was not observable through fetches.

## Key question 3: Service page anatomy

### Takeaway
Service pages on all three sites follow the same skeleton: H1 + one-line value proposition, "what is it", "why it matters / benefits", "how we help / process steps", FAQ accordion, downloads, related services, and a contact CTA that links to a central contact form rather than embedding a form. None of the three shows named contact persons with photos on the pages examined; TÜV NORD and TÜV Rheinland pages are ~900-1,000 words, TÜV SÜD's new template is shorter and uses an in-page anchor sub-nav.

### Cited Findings
- **TÜV Rheinland ISO 9001 page:** breadcrumbs; H1 "ISO 9001 Certification for Quality Management Systems" with a "Contact" button; two promo teasers (ISO 9001 revision, on-demand webinar "Watch now!"); intro "Put your trust in the renowned German provider TÜV Rheinland for your ISO 9001 certification" + "Contact us now"; "Why partner with us?" (4 points); "ISO 9001 Certification Process" (5 numbered steps from optional preliminary audit to re-certification within three years); FAQ accordion "Everything You Need to Know About ISO 9001 Certification" with "Show all"/"Hide all" and 8 questions; "Downloads and Assets around ISO 9001" (one 141 KB PDF); related cards "This might also interest you" (IATF 16949, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 50001, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 20000, each "discover more"); closing "Get in contact with us!" block. No embedded form; no named contact persons; no sidebar; body about 1,000 words. — [tuv.com ISO 9001](https://www.tuv.com/world/en/iso-9001-certification.html)
- **TÜV NORD ISO 9001 page:** breadcrumbs; H1 "ISO 9001 Certification" with photo and the ISO 9001 conformity mark; intro H2 "DIN EN ISO 9001: Quality management to ensure your success" + CTA "Request your ISO 9001 quote"; "Target group"; "Advantages: Why you benefit from ISO 9001 certification" (≈9 bullets + PDCA infographic); "The audit process for ISO 9001 certification" (7 numbered steps from "Enquiry, offer preparation & explanation" to "Continuous further development..."); "Integrated Management System (IMS)"; "Downloads" (one PDF, listed twice); "Frequently asked questions" (4 questions); "Discover your next service" (3 cards). No contact form on page; no named contacts; single column; ~900-1,000 words. — [tuev-nord.de ISO 9001](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/services/auditing-and-certification/iso-9001/)
- **TÜV NORD certification hub page** adds a "Questions & Answers" section with cost/requirements questions ("How much does certification cost?", "What are the requirements for certification?") and links to the "Certification Mark Download Centre" and "TÜV NORD CERT Download Area". — [tuev-nord.de certification hub](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/company/certification/)
- **TÜV SÜD service page (new template):** header shows an icon, parent label "Auditing", H1 "Social compliance audits", one-line summary, then an in-page anchor bar "Overview | Importance | Expertise | Contact us | Related resources"; sections "What are social compliance audits?", "Why social compliance audits are important" (3 bullets), "How TÜV SÜD can help you..." (mentions APSCA founder membership, ILO conventions), a CTA block "Get started with TÜV SÜD / Start your social compliance audit journey with us today. / Contact us", and "Related resources" (8 links). A sticky "Get in touch" button links to `/en/contact-us`. No FAQ, no downloads, no named person on this page. — [tuvsud.com social compliance audits](https://www.tuvsud.com/en/services/auditing/social-compliance-audits)
- **TÜV Rheinland CTA placement** repeats the contact CTA four times on one page (hero, after intro/benefits, mid-page, closing block), all pointing to `global-contact-form.jsp` with a `contact_id` parameter so the lead is routed to the right team. — [tuv.com ISO 9001](https://www.tuv.com/world/en/iso-9001-certification.html); [tuv.com UAE home](https://www.tuv.com/united-arab-emirates/en/)
- **TÜV NORD Middle East GMA page** uses "Request Your Individual Offer Now" (top) linking to `/me/en/request-offer-global-market-access/` and "Contact us" (bottom), and lists downloadable policy PDFs (Product Certification Policy, Impartiality Policy, Suspension and Withdrawal Procedures, Complaint and Appeal Handling Procedures, General Terms and Conditions). — [tuv-nord.com/me/en GMA](https://www.tuv-nord.com/me/en/services/global-market-access/)

### Inferences
- A reusable TIC service-page template could be: H1 + one-sentence promise; What/Why/How; numbered process; FAQ accordion (good for SEO and for the "humanised" tone the directors want); downloads (application forms, policies, brochures); related services; a single routed contact CTA. Named local contact persons would be a differentiator none of the three use on service pages.
- The "quote" CTA language differs: TÜV NORD says "Request your ISO 9001 quote"; TÜV Rheinland and TÜV SÜD say "Contact us". A quote-oriented CTA fits a smaller company that sells faster.

### Gaps
- Only one service page per site was examined in depth; variation across service families (e.g. testing lab pages, training pages with booking) was not checked.
- Whether any TÜV site shows named experts on pages other than leadership pages was not confirmed (none found on the pages visited).

## Key question 4: Certificate / certificate-holder verification tools

### Takeaway
Every TÜV runs public certificate verification, but at different depths: TÜV Rheinland's Certipedia is a full public registry (searchable by ID, browsable A-Z by company, with per-customer public pages and a test-mark misuse blacklist); TÜV NORD has a structured certificate database returning scope, validity and address; TÜV SÜD is fragmented (product certificate explorer for Product Service, and for management systems a per-subsidiary route, in the Middle East a simple number + CAPTCHA lookup at verify-tuvsudme.com).

### Cited Findings
- **Certipedia (TÜV Rheinland)** is described as "Certificate Database from TÜV Rheinland", publishing tested products, services, companies, systems and personnel certificates; each certificate has an "individual ID Number" and a "certificate and customer website link"; browsing is by "Product certificates" ("Product Manufacturers from A-Z"), "System certificates" ("Certified Companies from A-Z"), "Personnel certificates" ("Current Certification Programs"), functional safety / cybersecurity certificates (engineers, technicians, experts, CySec), and "Company certificates"; a "Black list test mark misuse" page exists; languages: Chinese (Simplified), English, German, Polish, Spanish; audience: consumers plus manufacturers, buyers and retailers; companies "receive a personal Certipedia page as independent public proof". — [certipedia.com](https://www.certipedia.com/)
- **Certipedia lookup is embedded on tuv.com homepages** (world and UAE): a field accepting "max 10 numbers", a "Find" button and a "Certipedia Detailed Search" link; Certipedia also appears in "Most Searched Terms" and footer "Handy Links". — [tuv.com/en](https://www.tuv.com/en); [tuv.com UAE home](https://www.tuv.com/united-arab-emirates/en/)
- **TÜV NORD certificate database** (`/en/certificate-database/`): modes "Search" and "Advanced search"; fields "Certificate number" (hint "Only consecutive digits, no hyphen or special characters"), "Company / organization" (required), "Standard" (required dropdown), "Location / City", "Country code (2 characters acc. ISO-3166-2)"; button "Search certificate". Result fields: Certificate Number, Norm, Valid Until, Company (up to three lines), Address, Report number / unique ID, Scope of certification, Valid From, followed by "We hereby confirm, that the above data is correct and refers to a valid certificate issued by TÜV NORD CERT GmbH." Error states: "No results found / Please check your input(s)." and "Certificate search could not be loaded". — [tuev-nord.de certificate database](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/certificate-database/)
- **TÜV NORD verification hub** (`/en/certificates-and-conformity-marks-verification-downloads/`) has four tools: management-system certificates (`/en/certificate-database/`), personal certificates (`/en/verify-tuev-nord-cert-personal-certificates/`), IT certificates "Find Certified Companies" (`/en/it-certificate/`), NDT personnel (`/en/ndt-certificate-verification/`), plus a "test mark download centre" (`/en/conformity-marks/`). The hub is linked from every footer as "Certificate database & marks of approval". — [tuev-nord.de verification hub](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/certificates-and-conformity-marks-verification-downloads/)
- TÜV NORD's own copy says the database needs only "the certificate number or the company name and the standard" and shows "which locations have been certified and until when the certificate is valid", with a warning that "in some cases data may still be incomplete (particularly customer address details)". — [search snippet of certificate database](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/certificate-database/)
- **TÜV SÜD "Certificate Finder"** page (Middle East version): heading "THE TÜV SÜD MARK - DEMONSTRATING COMMITMENT TO QUALITY AND SAFETY AND SUSTAINABILITY"; two routes: "DIRECTORY OF MANAGEMENT SYSTEM CERTIFICATES" (text says "you may send your enquiry through this form"; the link goes to `https://verify-tuvsudme.com/default`) and "TÜV SÜD PRODUCT SERVICE CERTIFICATE EXPLORER" ("Begin search on the TÜV SÜD Product Service certificate explorer" → `/en-ae/services/product-certification/ps-cert`; "Find out about the new security features of TÜV SÜD Product Service certificates"). A typo ("pelase") is live on the page. — [tuvsud.com/en-ae certificate finder](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/resource/certificate-finder)
- **verify-tuvsudme.com** (TÜV SÜD Middle East) asks "Please Enter Report / Certificate / ID Card Number", a CAPTCHA "Retype the characters from the picture:", and a "Submit!" button; no results schema or disclaimer visible; footer "About", "Imprint", "Privacy Policy". — [verify-tuvsudme.com](https://verify-tuvsudme.com/default)
- TÜV SÜD's certificate explorer is "the online window for certificates issued by TÜV SÜD Product Service" and can also look up Product Listing Scheme (PLS) products; some regional pages note TÜV SÜD "has intensified its procedure to track down quality mark and certificate counterfeiters". — [search snippets of certificate-finder pages](https://www.tuvsud.com/en-us/resource/certificate-finder)
- TÜV NORD's seal download centre is login-protected: "With a valid certificate from TÜV NORD CERT GmbH you are entitled to access your seals." — [search snippet](https://www.tuev-nord.de/de/pruefzeichen/)

### Inferences
- The minimum credible pattern for TIC: a public "Verify a certificate" page reachable from header/footer, searchable by certificate number (digits only, as TÜV NORD) and by company name + standard, returning holder, scope, sites, valid-from/valid-until and status, with an explicit confirmation sentence. A CAPTCHA-only number lookup (TÜV SÜD ME) is the weakest version seen.
- Certipedia's "company page" concept (a public profile per certified client, linkable from the client's own site, QR/ID on the certificate) is the strongest trust feature and directly relevant to CoC/Halal/ISO clients who want to prove certification to buyers.

### Gaps
- Could not view a live Certipedia record page or TÜV NORD result page with real data (placeholders only), so exact field order/labels on a result are unverified.
- `https://www.tuvsud.com/en/services/product-certification/ps-cert` returned 403 to fetchers and was not opened in the browser; the explorer's search fields are unverified.

## Key question 5: Government programme / export certification / PVoC pages

### Takeaway
TÜV Rheinland is the model here: a dedicated "Government Inspections & International Trade" (GIIT) hub with a country page per scheme (Iraq ICIGI, Egypt GOEIC/NFSA, Kuwait TIR, Qatar food CoC, Saudi SALEEM/SFDA/SQM, plus African PVoC programmes), each with a tab bar (Technical Information / Downloads / Contact us), structured fields (certificate validity, samples required, national deviations), a required-documents list, a regulated-products list, downloadable application/registration forms and fee structures, regulatory-update cards and recorded webinars.
