change the logo -- it documents it, and gives every office the same kit built around it.

03 · LOGO ANATOMY & CONSTRUCTION                                                                            03 LOGO  ONE UNIT: THE TILE
                                                                                                                     The logo is a lowercase tic built from square tiles --
HOW THE LOGO IS BUILT                                                                                                about 6.5 tiles wide and 4 tiles high, with the tagline
                                                                                                                     bar beneath. Every tile is cut on a diagonal into two
                                                                                                      6.5u           triangles. The unit u (one tile) drives every other
                                                                                                                     measurement in this book: clear space, minimum sizes,
              1u                                                                                                     the position of the company name.

TIC QUALITY CONTROL -- BRAND GUIDELINES 2026                                                                         THREE FILLS, ONE KEYLINE
                                                                                                                     Triangles are filled in three tones -- black, a graphite-
                                                                                                                     to-silver gradient and a white-to-silver gradient --
                                                                                                                     and sit on red squares that show as a TIC Red keyline.
                                                                                                                     The gradient is part of the artwork; the one-colour and
                                                                                                                     reversed versions (section 05) are generated from the
                                                                                                                     same paths for where a gradient cannot print.

                                                                                                                     THE I-DOT
                                                                                                                     A TIC Red rectangle inside a black frame, sitting above
                                                                                                                     the stem of the i. It is the only red-filled element in the
                                                                                                                     mark.

                                                                                                                     THE TAGLINE BAR
                                                                                                                     Two blocks under the tiles: a black block with T I C
                                                                                                                     letter-spaced, and a TIC Red block with Testing |
                                                                                                                     Inspection | Certification. The type (Myriad Pro Bold)
                                                                                                                     is outlined inside the artwork -- never retype it, and
                                                                                                                     never use Myriad anywhere else.

                                                                                                                     DO NOT REDRAW
                                                                                                                     Keyline weights vary slightly from tile to tile and the
                                                                                                                     black TIC block is a little taller than the red bar -- these
                                                                                                                     are part of the file. Always place the supplied files;
                                                                                                                     never rebuild or "tidy" the logo.
