Round 3 · polish brief T37

The photo and the first screen

One change of logic: the text sits beside the photo, never on it. The photo slot is now close to 3:2, the shape of the photos themselves, so the subject stays in the picture at every width. The rest follows from that.

  1. The editor marks the subject once (the Wagtail focal point). The template sets object-position from it.
  2. Left, centre or right: the subject stays in the picture, because the slot is as wide as the photo. See the three heads with the subject at the left, the centre and the right.
  3. Only the top and the bottom are cut on desktop (about a quarter of the height). The focal point decides which.
  4. Upload landscape 3:2, 1600 px wide or more. A portrait photo is not accepted for a head.

The header is one row: 115 px on desktop (was 190), 103 px on the phone (was 142). The logo stands alone; the company name stays in the page title and the footer. On the phone the utility bar is one row: Verify at the start, language and search at the end; Client area moves into the menu.

The intro in a head is 160 characters at most. Longer text is the first paragraph of the body.

4 · An empty photo place

  1. Card grids: all or none. If every card has a photo, the grid shows photos. If one is missing, the grid shows text cards. No grey plates.
  2. Program cards: the country tile. The country name is the picture. It needs no photo, now or later.
  3. The map: the address plate. City, address, “Open in maps”. No map script, so it loads in mainland China.
  4. Office photo, team photo, person photo: not drawn when empty. The text takes the width. A place that stands for a document (the sample report) keeps a quiet light plate with a dark caption.
  5. A head without a photo: the band head. The same text, the same action.
  6. A section with nothing to list is not drawn. No “No updates published yet.”

5 · A thin page

  1. The head and the facts bar are always there. They are the page when nothing else is.
  2. The template fills the body from linked data, in this order: services for this industry, offices, programs, insights. An empty one is not drawn.
  3. With one or two sections the body uses two columns, so the page is one screen and a half, not a strip.
  4. The red contact band never follows the head directly.

6 · For the engineer

  1. The drop uses container queries (860 px and 1180 px) so the phone boards can sit on the canvas. On the site use the same rules as media queries, or keep them.
  2. New classes: .ph (.hero, .band), .fb, .jump, .tcard, .pcard, .ct, .addr. All use logical properties; RTL needs no extra rule.
  3. The facts bar is data, not copy: each page type has four fixed cells. A cell without a value keeps its bracket until an owner fills it.
  4. The body of the service page and the program page is as in round 1. Only the head, the facts bar and the empty places change.
