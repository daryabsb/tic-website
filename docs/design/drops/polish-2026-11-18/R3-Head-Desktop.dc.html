Iraq Certificate of Conformity

Many products imported into Iraq need a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) before customs clearance. COSQC, Iraq’s Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control, appointed TIC Quality Control and LOX to inspect, test and certify them.
