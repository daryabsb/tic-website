03 · Rules our staff follow

  • Inspectors are paid in a way that does not depend on the result of an inspection.
  • No inspector inspects goods they designed, supplied, installed or own.
  • Gifts, hospitality and other inducements are declared and refused where they could influence a decision.

05 · Group companies and subcontracting

Shared ownership of TIC Quality Control, Seacorr and LOX does not influence any inspection or certification decision. When TIC engages a group company or another subcontractor for part of an inspection or for laboratory testing, we tell the client in advance, we use only competent and, where required, accredited subcontractors, and TIC remains responsible for the result. The partner laboratories we use are named on each testing page.
