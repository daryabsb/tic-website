How inspection is done at TIC

  • Type A inspection body under ISO/IEC 17020 — independent of the parties we inspect for (ANAB certificate AI-3314)
  • Sampling to ISO 2859-1 unless the contract asks for 100 % inspection
  • A signed report with photographs, usually the same or the next working day, with a certificate number you can verify online
  • Own inspectors from ten offices; named partners elsewhere, told to you in advance
