Iraq Certificate of Conformity

Most goods imported into Iraq need a Certificate of Conformity (CoC) before customs clearance. TIC Quality Control, in partnership with LOX, is appointed by COSQC (Iraq’s Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control) to inspect, test and certify them.

01 · The regulator and our appointment

Appointed by COSQC

The Iraq CoC program is run by COSQC under the ICIGI (Iraqi Conformity Import Goods Inspection) scheme. TIC Quality Control and LOX were appointed by contract dated 22 May 2024 for a three-year term; certificates have been issued since September 2024.

06 · Testing, inspection and labelling

What the inspector checks

  • Conformity of each product with the applicable Iraqi standard (IQS) or accepted international standard
  • Arabic labelling and the country-of-origin mark
  • Quantity, packing and marking against the invoice and packing list
  • Sampling and testing where required, through TIC or named partner laboratories
