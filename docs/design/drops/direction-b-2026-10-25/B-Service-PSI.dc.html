01 · What it is

An independent look before the goods leave

Our inspector visits the factory, warehouse or port when the order is ready, draws a sample by an agreed sampling plan and checks it against your purchase order, the applicable standard and the approved sample. You receive a report with photographs the same or the next working day.

02 · Who needs it

When it is required or wise

  • Required by a Certificate of Conformity program before shipment to Iraq, Egypt, Botswana or Libya
  • Required by a letter of credit, a GAFTA contract or a buyer’s purchase terms
  • Wise for a first order from a new supplier, or a product with a history of claims
