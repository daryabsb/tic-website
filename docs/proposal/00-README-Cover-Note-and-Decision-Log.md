## A. Read me first

### A.1 What this round is

This is a proposal, not a design and not a build. Nothing has been drawn, no code has been written, no agency has been chosen. The aim of this round is to agree what the new site should say and do before anyone spends money on how it looks or what it runs on. You read and comment, I merge your replies and revise, and only then do we seek approval to start design.

What I need from you, in one line: read the documents for your role (section A.3 tells you which and how long they take), write in the comment boxes or reply by email quoting section numbers, and answer the questionnaire (02) for your office or department.
