# Staging review, Nov 22, 2026: the information TIC still owes

Darya hands staging to the directors and the office managers. This folder is the record
of what they were asked.

- `TIC-website-information-we-need.docx`: the file sent with the email. 539 items in 23
  parts: parts 1 to 14 are the offices, parts 15 to 23 the departments. Each row has a
  number (for example `CAI-03`), the page, what the site shows now, what is needed, and an
  empty answer column.
- `gaps.md`: the same list as plain text.

## How the list was made

1. A read-only scan of the staging database and of every rendered English page (124
   pages: 91 live, 33 drafts): every text in square brackets, every empty photo place,
   every sample photo caption, the people, accreditation, download and routing rows.
2. Six review agents, one per site section (offices, about and trust, services and
   training, industries, programs and home, news), read the page texts for the gaps that
   are not in brackets: missing facts, facts nobody confirmed, conflicts between pages,
   draft pages, text faults. 572 findings; rows that ask the same thing for many pages are
   one row in a department part (539 rows).
3. Check: every placeholder of the scan is named by a review item, except six on the
   draft Privacy and Terms pages, which the "draft page" rows of Legal cover.

Photos were judged from their alt texts and captions, not from the pictures.

## Site faults the review found (corrected in the same pull request)

- The English Baghdad page listed the Iraq program two times (English and Arabic page).
- The red contact band said "WhatsApp" on pages with no WhatsApp (Guangzhou, D11).
- The contents list of an article showed `&amp;` for `&`.
